# Business And Personal Use Cases

## Individual / Personal

Use for daily purchases, weekly reviews, savings rate tracking, and category breakdowns.

Example prompts:

- "Initialize my monthly NGN budget with 500000 income and 300000 spending limit."
- "Add lunch for 15000 under Food and show whether I am still safe."
- "Summarize my spending this week."

Returns local state, expense entries, guardian warnings, and savings rate.

## Freelancer

Use for per-client budget tracking, project expenses, and onchain invoice evidence.

Example prompts:

- "Track a 200 USD software subscription for Client A."
- "Check if a 700 USD contractor payment will exceed my project budget."
- "Log this invoice expense to Pharos as proof for my client."

Returns project spending, warning codes, and optional Pharos transaction data.

## Small Business

Use for payroll, vendors, utilities, marketing, and monthly profit-and-loss snapshots.

Example prompts:

- "Add payroll of 1200000 under Payroll."
- "Audit this month before I approve another vendor payment."
- "Log the vendor payment onchain for audit trail."

Returns expense totals, category breakdowns, and immutable ledger hashes.

## DAO Treasury

Use for agent-managed treasury limits, transparent spending, and multi-sig coordination.

Example prompts:

- "Check whether this grant payment fits the treasury budget."
- "Log a 5000 USDC contributor payment to Pharos."
- "Show all onchain expense hashes for the treasury signer."

Returns safe/unsafe decisions, warning codes, and Pharos transaction links.

## E-Commerce Store

Use for inventory costs, ad spend, revenue tracking, and monthly operating budget reviews.

Example prompts:

- "Add 900 USD inventory restock under Vendor."
- "Add 250 USD campaign spend under Marketing."
- "Show spending by category and recent expenses."

Returns totals, category-level spend, recent expenses, and budget utilization.

## NGO / Nonprofit

Use for donor fund allocation, transparent reporting, and auditable field expenses.

Example prompts:

- "Track 100000 NGN food distribution under Other with a donor note."
- "Warn me if the next field payment exceeds the monthly program budget."
- "Record this expenditure onchain for public transparency."

Returns local and onchain evidence suitable for downstream reporting.
